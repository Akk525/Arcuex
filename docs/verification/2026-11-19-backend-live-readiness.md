## Evidence boundary and remaining dependencies

- Operator approval must identify a real Arc lending contract whose runtime matches the published profile. For this version, the contract must emit the configured `CoveredLiquidation` event on a successful direct transaction and transfer the exact seized testnet USDC amount to the liquidator. Historical bytecode at the event block must match the published code hash. A proxy with separately mutable behavior needs a separately reviewed source profile.
- Appoint independent replay and recovery authorities, with separate Ed25519 keys and verifiable appointment provenance. Put only approved public keys in `ARCUEX_INDEPENDENT_LIQUIDATION_PROFILE`. Both sign the exact canonical JSON body under role-separated messages via `independentAttestationMessage`. The body binds chain, case, incident, exposure, policy digest, canonical block/transaction/log, replay and accounting artifact digests, and a recovery cutoff. Keep the source artifacts accessible by digest for human audit. The replay party must reproduce the counterfactual threshold crossing and exclude other causes. The recovery party must resolve recoveries and prior compensation through the declared cutoff. This version allows a five-minute accounting freshness window.
- A qualifying incident must occur on the covered position while the same genuinely funded policy is active and satisfy the waiting period and chronology checks. Net insured loss is constrained by seized collateral minus extinguished debt, recoveries, prior compensation, the deductible, the policy limit and same-loss commitments. Neither fixture consistency nor model output authorizes financial execution.
- The deployed pool enforces payout at or before the coverage end timestamp. It does not enforce backend quote expiry, revoked delegation, or evidence deadlines in calldata. A late bind may still collect a premium onchain while remaining unrecognized by the backend. A claims-authority wallet can submit already-issued calldata outside the backend's verification deadline. A controlled signing workflow must enforce the current approved evidence and `signBefore` at authorization and submission. Hard onchain enforcement would require a reviewed contract change and deployment. Never submit a transaction merely to test the path.
- An interrupted bind can be linked by the existing confirmed-log indexer when the original immutable records and valid grant survive. After a payout callback is lost, the authority can resubmit the same hash. Settlement anchor scans cover payments recorded with an anchor. Historical payments without anchors require a receipt audit, and unknown payout hashes still require operator recovery. RPC outage or failed validation keeps existing commitments held.

## Frontend handoff

1. Discover `/api/procurement/products` and `/api/procurement/capacity`. Use the displayed version, timestamp and source. Quote `capacityStatus` is advisory and the digest covers the immutable quote terms alone.
2. Use the existing wallet session and owner/delegation flows. Accept the exact quote digest, prepare `approve` then `bind` as separate payments, and request external wallet approval for each. Register the approval hash and submit the bind hash to `confirm`. On `UNCONFIRMED` or timeout, retry the existing hash. For a reverted bind, refresh capacity and allowance and retry only inside the original quote window. Obtain a new quote at expiry. Use `/api/policies/<id>/lifecycle` for canonical linkage and active coverage.
3. Keep case and policy IDs stable from claim intake through reimbursement. Show independent verification and its expiry before human review. Keep reviewer and authority credentials on the server or in an authorized operator session. Settlement preparation returns unsigned data and `signBefore`. The operator must get fresh confirmation before external signing and submission. Only surface payment after the server confirms the receipt. Reverted transactions use `mark_failed` with the confirmed hash. A wallet rejection without a hash leaves the claim pending.
4. Show fixture mode, confirmation pending, reorged, unavailable and failed states. An orphaned payment returns to pending and keeps the original timeline entry for audit.
